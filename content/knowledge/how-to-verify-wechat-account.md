# How to Verify a WeChat Account: Step-by-Step Guide for International Users

WeChat may require additional verification when you create an account, sign in from a new device, change your phone number, or trigger a security review. Depending on your region and account history, the process may include SMS authentication, a security CAPTCHA, or friend-assisted QR code verification.

This guide explains how to complete the official verification steps safely and what options are available if you do not have an eligible WeChat contact who can help.

## Before You Start: Verification Prerequisites

Prepare the following before starting registration or recovery:

- Install the latest official WeChat app from the Apple App Store or Google Play.
- Use a direct SIM card that can receive SMS messages. Avoid temporary, public, or shared numbers.
- Confirm that your phone number is entered with the correct country code.
- Use a stable mobile or home internet connection.
- Turn off VPNs, proxy services, and unusual network-routing tools temporarily.
- Keep your phone’s date and time set automatically.
- Use your normal device whenever possible.
- Have access to the email address or phone number associated with the account, if applicable.
- Ask a trusted, eligible WeChat contact for help only if WeChat specifically requests friend verification.

Avoid repeatedly submitting registration attempts. Multiple failed attempts can cause additional security checks or temporary restrictions.

## Step 1: Complete Phone Registration and the Security CAPTCHA

1. Open the official WeChat app.
2. Select **Sign Up** or begin the account recovery process.
3. Choose your country or region.
4. Enter your mobile number, including the correct international dialing code.
5. Accept the applicable terms and privacy notices.
6. Complete the security CAPTCHA or slider challenge.
7. Request the SMS verification code.
8. Enter the code exactly as received.
9. Create a strong password that you do not reuse on other services.

If the SMS does not arrive:

- Wait several minutes before requesting another code.
- Check that your SIM can receive international or service messages.
- Confirm that your carrier has not blocked short-code or automated messages.
- Do not request codes repeatedly in quick succession.
- Try the process later from the same trusted network and device.

After the phone check, WeChat may display a message asking for friend-assisted verification.

## Step 2: Pass WeChat Friend QR Code Verification

Friend verification is an anti-abuse measure. WeChat decides which accounts can act as verifiers, and eligibility can vary by country, account status, account age, and current security policy.

### Verifier Eligibility

The person helping you should meet the requirements shown in their WeChat app. In general, WeChat may require that the verifier:

- Has an established WeChat account in good standing.
- Has completed the account’s applicable security or identity checks.
- Is not currently restricted, suspended, or under review.
- Has not exceeded WeChat’s verification assistance limit.
- Is located in a region accepted by the verification flow.
- Has not recently helped too many other accounts.
- Is using the official WeChat app.

The exact requirements are controlled by WeChat and can change. If the app rejects a verifier, try another trusted contact who meets the on-screen requirements.

### Scanning Steps for the Verifier

1. Start the verification flow on your device until the QR code appears.
2. Send the QR code screen to your trusted verifier through a private channel, or have them scan it directly from your screen.
3. The verifier opens WeChat on their own device.
4. They use WeChat’s **Scan** feature to scan your QR code.
5. They follow the prompts to confirm that they are helping your registration or account recovery.
6. You complete any remaining confirmation steps on your device.
7. Wait for WeChat to confirm whether verification succeeded.

Do not share your password, SMS code, recovery code, or account credentials with the verifier. A verifier should only scan and confirm the official in-app request.

## Step 3: Options If You Do Not Have an Eligible Friend

If you do not have a trusted contact who can complete friend verification, use only official or clearly scoped assistance channels.

### Option A: Check for Payment Card Verification

Some accounts or regions may display an alternative verification method involving a supported payment card or billing detail.

If this option appears:

1. Read the on-screen instructions carefully.
2. Use only your own valid payment method.
3. Make sure the billing country and card details match the requested information.
4. Never send a full card number, CVV, password, or one-time banking code to an individual.
5. Stop if the app does not offer this method or if the request appears outside the official WeChat flow.

Availability varies. A payment-card option is not guaranteed for every account, country, or verification attempt.

### Option B: Contact Official WeChat Support

If the available methods fail, use WeChat’s official Help or Feedback channels. Include:

- Your country or region.
- The phone number format and carrier type, without exposing unnecessary personal data.
- The exact error message.
- Screenshots with passwords and security codes hidden.
- The device model and operating system.
- Whether you are registering, signing in, or recovering an account.

Do not send your password or SMS verification code to anyone claiming to represent support through an unofficial channel.

### Option C: Use Scoped Verification Assistance

If you need help understanding the official QR verification flow, a scoped assistance service may be able to explain the steps and help coordinate an eligible verifier. Any assistance should be limited to the verification action requested by WeChat.

Avoid services that:

- Promise guaranteed approval.
- Ask for your password or SMS code.
- Sell “aged,” “verified,” or pre-made accounts.
- Request unrestricted access to your device.
- Ask you to bypass regional or platform security controls.

## Troubleshooting Common Verification Errors

| Error message | Common causes | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| **Security Risk** | VPN or proxy use, unusual device activity, repeated attempts, an untrusted number, or inconsistent location signals | Turn off VPNs and proxies, use the official app, wait before retrying, and continue from your usual device and network. |
| **QR Code Expired** | The QR code was left open too long, was captured too early, or was scanned after the session changed | Return to the verification screen and generate a new QR code. Have the verifier scan it immediately. |
| **Verification Limit Reached** | The verifier has helped too many accounts recently or has reached an account-specific limit | Wait and try later with another eligible, trusted verifier. Do not repeatedly submit failed requests. |
| **SMS Code Not Received** | Carrier filtering, incorrect country code, weak signal, or too many code requests | Confirm the number and signal, wait several minutes, and request one new code only. |
| **Verifier Not Eligible** | The contact does not meet WeChat’s current account, region, or security requirements | Review the in-app requirements and use a different established account in good standing. |
| **Unable to Verify** | A temporary platform review, device issue, or incomplete recovery information | Update WeChat, restart the process later, and contact official support if the issue continues. |

## Platform Safety and Policy Warning

Do not buy fake, pre-made, rented, or “guaranteed verified” WeChat accounts. These accounts may be:

- Created with stolen or recycled phone numbers.
- Linked to someone else’s identity or payment information.
- Compromised before delivery.
- Reclaimed by the original owner.
- Restricted because of suspicious activity.
- In violation of WeChat’s terms or local law.

Using an account that you do not legitimately control can lead to permanent loss of access, privacy exposure, and financial risk. The safest approach is to create or recover your own account through the official WeChat app and complete the verification steps requested by the platform.

## Conclusion

WeChat verification is designed to prevent automated abuse and unauthorized account access. Start with a direct SIM, the official app, a stable connection, and no VPN or proxy. Complete SMS and CAPTCHA checks carefully, then follow the friend QR verification instructions if WeChat requests them.
